# Provider construction services

Providers use **Add your service**, select **Construction**, and choose an
approved catalog task. The form shows the required qualification; new trades
do not prefill a price or invent sample certified providers. Providers enter
their own indicative fixed/daily/negotiable rate and discuss the final contract
with the customer through the existing messaging flow.

- Masonry, Concrete Block Laying, Bricklaying, and Wall Plastering require
  **Masonry NC II**.
- Construction Carpentry requires **Carpentry NC II** or **Carpentry NC III**,
  using the existing carpentry certificate policy.
- Tile Installation and Tile Repair require **Tile Setting NC II**.
- Construction Painting requires **Construction Painting NC II**.

The provider must have verified identity and a current matching certificate
approved by the System Administrator through the TESDA Registry of Certified
Workers before submitting for publication. Listings still require administrator
approval. Pending, expired, withdrawn, unrelated, or undocumented certificates
do not qualify for public discovery, inquiry creation, or booking. The supported
qualification lists drive both the provider certificate form and server-side
certificate review. No existing certificate or listing is automatically approved.

The catalog describes specific work within a trade qualification, not a general
contractor, professional design, engineering, or an unsupported licence claim.
The platform verifies a worker's certificate; it does not represent the service
or NeighborHelp as accredited by TESDA.
