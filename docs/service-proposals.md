# Provider service proposals

Providers may propose a service name, a suggested category, its scope and a current
TESDA certificate already authenticated by an administrator. Existing catalog
service names must use the catalog instead. Other TESDA NC qualifications can be
uploaded using the certification form's additional qualification option; they
still require a registry check before they can support a proposal.

An active administrator, including required MFA, reviews the proposed scope,
confirms the qualification matches and checks that the work does not require an
unsupported professional licence. Review notes are required for both decisions.
Approval creates a provider-bound authorization and a private draft listing.
The provider adds pricing and submits the listing for the ordinary listing review.
Proposal approval does not publish a service. Rejected proposals display feedback
and may be revised. A deleted draft may be restored without copying its approval
to a different listing or provider.

Authorization is checked against a current certificate of the approved qualification
when messaging, booking, amending and starting work. Certificate renewal may use
a newly authenticated entry of that same qualification. Providers cannot write
proposal decisions or authorization records. The rules bind custom titles,
categories and service IDs to the approved authorization. Approved listings appear
in customer search and live category dialogs; draft proposals never appear there.

The admin bell opens an activity inbox. New registrations, pending certificate
submissions, identity results, pending service listings, service proposals and
reports generate idempotent in-app alerts. Selecting an alert opens its review
page and records the read receipt. The existing notification campaign sender
remains under Manage. These admin activity alerts do not initiate device pushes.
Only events occurring after deployment create these new admin alerts.
